I am a postdoctoral researcher in sports economics at the University of Tübingen. My research interests are sports economics and management, behavioral economics, and applied econometrics. In particular, I use professional sports as a setting to study economic behavior. My current research projects focus on integrity issues in sports, bias and favoritism in subjective performance evaluations, and performance in contests.
